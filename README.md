### 👋 About Me

I'm a **Computer Engineer at Lockheed Martin** and a May 2026 graduate of **Cal Poly Pomona** (B.S. Computer Engineering, 3.80 GPA). I work where hardware meets software: embedded systems, autonomous navigation, and the power systems that keep drones in the air.

I spent two years on Cal Poly Pomona's Lockheed Martin-funded UAV team, first writing autonomous flight code and then leading the guidance, navigation and control (G.N.C) team for SUAS 2026. Along the way I picked up a habit of building tools that make engineering work faster, like the flight-log viewer below.
